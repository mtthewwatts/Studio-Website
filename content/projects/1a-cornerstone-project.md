At the end of my first term I, along with three other students in my program, conceptualized, mechanically designed with EV3 Lego Bricks, and programmed with RobotC, a robot that pathed itself through a model city, detected an infrared beacon, sprayed water using a Dollar Store spray bottle, then returned home.

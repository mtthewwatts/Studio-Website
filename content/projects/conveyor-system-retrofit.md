Conveyor Retrofit — Ideas Clinic.

## Challenge

The existing industrial conveyor system was unreliable, difficult to maintain, and caused frequent downtime, limiting its effectiveness as a hands-on teaching tool for Management Engineering students.

## Solution

Redesigned and iterated 4+ CAD models of the conveyor diverter arm, culminating in a robust pneumatic subassembly integrating custom 3D-printed components and a solenoid valve. Implemented a custom HMI to enable interactive lab use and seamless system integration.

## Learning

Gained practical experience in iterative mechanical design, pneumatic system integration, and HMI development, while learning how to design for reliability, maintainability, and real-world industrial constraints. The final solution reduced maintenance time by 95% and significantly improved system uptime and instructional efficiency.

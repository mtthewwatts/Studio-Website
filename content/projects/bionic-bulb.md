**AESTHETIC DESIGN   |   PROJECT MANAGEMENT**

Bionic Bulb (work in progress).

![Breadboard prototype of the Bionic Bulb electronics](/projects/bionic-bulb/img00.png)

![Concept renders and the assembled sculpture](/projects/bionic-bulb/img01.png)

Designed a sound-responsive kinetic flower sculpture using a MAX9814 microphone, Arduino-controlled stepper motor, and MOSFET-driven LEDs to create real-time interactive motion and lighting.

Through this project, I learned how to integrate mechanical design, embedded control, and aesthetic intent, developing user-focused interactive systems that balance expressive motion, electrical constraints, and reliable actuation.
